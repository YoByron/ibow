ibow

Intelligent Business Orchestration and Workflows. The front where customers order on their own phone, the back where staff do the work and take the money, and the brain beside the owner that knows the numbers. No hardware to buy, no training for staff.

Try the live demo Source on GitHub

Customers: the front

A QR code on the counter opens the menu in the customer's browser. No app, no account, no phone number. They order, pay by UPI QR or at the counter, and follow a live token from received to ready.

Staff: the back

The counter confirms cash and UPI (money is never shown as received until a person confirms it) and calls tokens. Each station sees only its own items, totalled across orders and spoken as one batch.

Owners: the brain

Today in plain sentences, orders kept apart from money actually received, the busiest hour, best sellers, a fair comparison with yesterday and tomorrow's prep forecast. It is all rule-based and needs no AI. An optional analyst answers questions on the owner's own model key.

Themes: your look, not ours

Colours, typeface, corners, header, logo, icons, photos and every word come from configuration or the owner's Appearance screen. Restyling a business never needs a code change.

Consultancy and support

ibow is free. Setup help, custom workflows, integrations and support are a paid service from its author: LinkedIn or Instagram.
